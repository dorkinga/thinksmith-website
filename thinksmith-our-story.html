Our story

Built by someone who never learned the way school expected.

ThinkSmith didn't start as a business plan. It started with a kid who was told he wasn't smart — and a researcher who later proved that wrong, for 1,742 students at a time.

Leslie Richards
Co-Founder & Creator of ThinkSmith

I dropped out at 16 because school was built for rote learners. ThinkSmith is built for the rest of us.

I struggled with dyslexia, failed two grades, and spent years believing I wasn't smart. It wasn't true — I just didn't learn the way school expected. Most kids don't.

Later, I led a study with 1,742 university students that proved something life-changing: when learners engage with new ideas before instruction, their confidence and performance skyrocket.

Most children cannot absorb brand-new information during a lesson unless they have some prior foothold — and repetition alone doesn't fix that.

Homeschooling already gives your child the freedom to learn differently. ThinkSmith gives them the structure to thrive.

— Leslie Richards, Co-Founder & Creator of ThinkSmith
